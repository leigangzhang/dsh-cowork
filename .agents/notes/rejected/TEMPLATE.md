# Rejected: <topic>

> Copy this file to `.agents/notes/rejected/<yyyy-mm-dd>-<module>-<slug>.md`. A rejected record is a **proposal frozen at the moment it lost**: the verdict is the reason it is kept, and the body stays as it was proposed — do not rewrite it into a retrospective.

## Problem

The motivation, written to stand without the solution. One paragraph a reader can check against the current repository.

## Proposal

The change that was proposed, in the tense it was proposed in. Keep the interface, acceptance criteria, and plan that were on the table; a rejection is only useful if the reader can see exactly what lost.

## Alternatives considered

Every genuine alternative and why it lost, one paragraph per alternative. Empty is a defect: a rejection with no alternatives is an opinion, not a record.

## Why rejected

The verdict in full sentences: which evidence, constraint, or cost decided it, and what would have to change for a future proposal to win. Say what the rejection **rules out**, not just what it declined.

> Keep this file only while it prevents a tempting mistake. Once the rationale no longer stops anyone from re-proposing the same thing, delete the record — a rejected idea that lingers becomes a fact in the next reader's context.
